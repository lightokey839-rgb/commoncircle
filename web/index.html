          Good to know before you lend or borrow

          • Unsecured. No collateral, no interest, and repayment cannot be forced. A borrower may not repay.

              Lending

              Loans

              Money goes straight from the lender to the borrower. The circle contract checks the loan and passes the USDC on in the same transaction; it does not keep a pot.

              Ask your circle

              Post what you need and why in the circle chat. Members offer to lend; you choose who to borrow from. The card is off-chain until you choose and sign.

              Ask one member directly

              You already know who to ask. The request is created on-chain straight away and names them; only they can fund it.

              Loan lifecycle

              1. OpenWaits for the named lender until the funding deadline. The borrower can cancel; unfunded requests lapse.
              2. FundedThe borrower has the USDC. The due time starts now.
              3. RepaidPaid back straight to the lender. On time builds the passport; late is recorded and pauses Trusted perks until cleared.

              Lending

              Ask one member directly

              Choose a member, the amount and how long. Your wallet creates the request on-chain naming them as lender; only they can fund it, before the funding deadline. Prefer to ask everyone? Use the circle chat.

              Check before you sign

              • Creating the request moves no USDC. It costs a small NEAR storage deposit, shown by your wallet.
              • When the lender funds it, the USDC goes straight to you and the due time starts.
              • You repay the lender directly. No interest. Repaying late is recorded on your passport.
              • The loan is unsecured. Nobody can force repayment, and nobody has to lend.

              My passport

              Governance · Sputnik DAO

              Circle treasury

              A shared treasury run by a Sputnik DAO. It lends only to Trusted members who are not stewards, and only when both stewards approve. Repayments go back to the DAO.

              No verified treasury

              This circle has no DAO attached, or its DAO did not pass this app's trust checks, so treasury actions stay hidden.

              How far to trust this

              DAO verified by CommonCircle frontend trust checks. This app checks the account runs the pinned Sputnik DAO code, has no access keys, and that its voting rules need the circle's stewards. The circle contract itself does not check which DAO is attached.

              In this testnet demo both steward accounts may be run by the same operator: two accounts are not proof of two people.

              Ask the treasury for a loan

              Step 1 records your request on-chain. Step 2 sends it to the stewards as a DAO proposal (with a refundable bond). The money arrives when both approve.

                    Zero custody: how a loan moves

                    1Lendersends USDC with ft_transfer_call
                    2Circle contractchecks loan, lender, token, amount and gas
                    3Borrowerreceives the USDC in the same transaction

                    The contract holds the tokens only for the moment it forwards them. If any check fails (wrong token, unregistered borrower, too little gas…), the lender is refunded. At rest its USDC balance should be zero.
