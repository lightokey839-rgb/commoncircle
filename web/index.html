Relationship-based lending on NEAR

Ajo pays you in turn. CommonCircle is for when it isn't your turn.

Members lend USDC straight to each other, with no interest and no pooled pot. Every on-time repayment is written into a passport NFT that cannot be sold or sent; it turns Trusted, which raises your limit and opens the circle's DAO treasury.

              NFTs That Do Something

              Your repayment history becomes your financial passport

              A non-transferable NEP-171 token, one per member per circle. Repayments write its tier; the tier sets your credit limit and treasury access. The picture is drawn by the contract from the circle's theme and your record.

              Shared treasury · Sputnik DAO

              The circle's DAO treasury

              It pays out only when both stewards vote yes, and only to Trusted members who are not stewards. The money goes straight to the borrower and comes back to the DAO on repayment.

              Trust note: the app checks that this account runs the pinned Sputnik DAO code and has no access keys (DAO verified by frontend trust checks). The circle contract does not check this itself. In this testnet demo both steward accounts are run by the same operator, so the two votes do not prove two independent people.
